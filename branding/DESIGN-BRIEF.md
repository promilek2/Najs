# Najs visual identity brief

Status: `INTEGRATED_FOR_REVIEW`

The Najs Fold identity now includes a canonical vector mark, monochrome mark,
wallpaper, terminal mark, and desktop color defaults. The ISO build renders the
wallpaper and icon sizes from their vector sources so the actual outputs can be
reviewed in Plasma, SDDM, Konsole, and the application launcher.

Direction: clean and lightly futuristic, strong silhouette at 16 px, restrained
dark neutral surfaces with one high-chroma accent, and no imitation of Windows,
macOS, SteamOS, ChromeOS, or stock Plasma. The identity must work in monochrome,
terminal, boot, installer, and high-DPI contexts before inclusion in an ISO.

The fold in the continuous N represents one system moving between explicit
generations. Palette: midnight `#07111C`, surface `#102538`, and Najs cyan
`#59D2FF`.
